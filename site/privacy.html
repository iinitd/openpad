Privacy policy

Effective 11 November 2026

Puff is a client app. It stores your workspace on your device and talks only to the servers you configure. The developer does not operate a service that receives your documents, conversations, or connection credentials.

What Puff stores on your device

  • Connection profiles, including server addresses, passwords, and tokens you enter.
  • Preferences, such as prompts, recent projects, document positions, and preview state.
  • Places where you left off, so the app can restore the same view next time.

This information lives in the app's local storage. It is not uploaded to the developer. Removing the app deletes it from the device.

What Puff sends over the network

  • Requests to the Paseo, OpenClaw, or OpenCode servers you add: the messages, file content, and commands you ask the app to send.
  • The connection handshake, including the password or token and, for OpenClaw, a device identity key that proves this device to that gateway.
  • Images you attach to a message, sent to the server handling that conversation.

Those servers are run by you or by someone you chose. Their handling of your data is governed by their configuration and policies, not by this one.

Photos

Puff asks for photo library access only when you attach an image to a message. It reads the image you pick and does not scan or index the rest of your library.

Local network

Puff asks for local network access so it can reach a server running on your computer or another device on the same network. The permission is used only for the connections you configure.

What Puff does not do

  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • No account creation and no developer-side user profile.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with third parties.

Security

Connection credentials are kept in the app's sandbox. Traffic to a server uses the transport that server provides; a server reachable only over plain HTTP or WS cannot offer transport encryption, so prefer HTTPS or WSS when the server is not on your own network.

Children

Puff is a general-purpose writing tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it, and the current version stays available at this address.
